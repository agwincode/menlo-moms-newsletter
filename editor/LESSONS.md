# Lessons from sent issues

The editor appends here each Friday after comparing the last sent issue (Gmail, from
menlomoms@substack.com) with its draft. Newest at the bottom. When a lesson becomes a
standing rule, move it into RULES.md or STYLE.md.

## Baseline: 9 issues, Aug 28 – Oct 30, 2026 (drafts vs. what Angela sent)

**Selection**
- About 70% of drafted lineup cards survive (46 of 66). Sent lineups run 5–6 cards (range 2–8).
- Cut every single week: far-future festival watchlist items (BottleRock 2027, Maker Faire 2027,
  Outside Lands 2027, the OFJCC Israel trip). They never ran.
- Cut: adult-oriented programs: wine tastings and estate tours (Filoli Reserve, Taste of Filoli),
  adult classes (floral design, vegetable gardening, native garden design, compost), evening talks
  (Canopy x Amy Stewart), after-parties, Hops and Props, a teen college fair, a highbrow teen
  concert (Let the Poets Speak).
- Cut: most Midpen docent hikes (Arachnophilia, Mid-Autumn Moon Hike, Wonders of Cherry Springs,
  New Trails at La Honda). Holiday-themed or easy family ones survive as quick hits
  (Halloween & Hiking Hounds, Sunset Serenity Hike, Evening Explorations at Alpine Pond).
- Cut or demoted: the zoo's repeating encounters (Feed the Flamingo, Meet a Bald Eagle).
- Added or promoted by Angela: big public festivals and spectacles, even beyond the core towns:
  San Carlos Art & Wine Faire, Oktoberfest Redwood City, Palo Alto Moonlight Run, Mavericks Surf
  Festival, TechFest, the Pumpkin Weigh-Off, Woodside Day of the Horse, Art for All Family Day
  (Palo Alto Art Center), Fall Family Day.
- Added as quick hits: neighborhood traditions from InMenlo (Fire Station 4 haunted house,
  The Members' 20th Halloween show), preschool carnivals, Foster City and Pillar Point Halloween
  events, library storytimes with a twist (Global Language Storytime).
- Extra sections she uses when they fit: "📅 Mark Your Calendar" (a midweek event right after
  the weekend), "👀 Next Weekend" (two lines on the following weekend's headliners),
  "✈️ Continuing in SF: Fleet Week" (a regional spectacle), "💡 Editorial Shoutout" (a new
  kid-friendly local spot, e.g. Camper), "🌿 More Ways to Fill the Long Weekend" (holiday weekends).
- Card add-ons she writes: "Pro move:" (shuttles, arrive-early tips, wristband bundles) and
  "Heads up:" (flood advisory, a tragedy that's better for older kids). Both sourced, one line.

**Photos**
- Every lineup card gets a photo (47 of 50).
- Her usual source is a screenshot of the organizer page's hero image or flyer (most photos are
  wide PNG crops like 1774×754 or 1800×462). Next: the organizer's own photo (Filoli 320×240,
  Springline Oktoberfest). When the organizer has nothing usable: a news or Wikipedia photo
  (Almanac, InMenlo, a bald eagle), and once a generic seasonal illustration.
- No headshots of named people. Book covers are fine.
