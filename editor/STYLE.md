# Format and voice

Brand: **Menlo Weekend Survival Guide** by @menlomoms. A Menlo parent writing to Menlo and
Peninsula parents (kids 0–12; Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Redwood City, Atherton, Portola Valley,
Woodside, San Carlos). Sends Thursday morning.

## Voice
- Breezy, helpful, opinionated. Reads like a text from a friend who knows the area.
- "We," never "I." No em dashes (use commas, periods or colons).
- No AI tropes ("Don't be x, be y," "Unlike x, it's y").
- **Keep the voice:** playful card openers, a warm hook, personality lines like
  "this kind of arts-plus-making is exactly what we look for," and "genuinely"/"actually"
  where Angela would say them.
- **Cut:** whole weak items; secondary factual clutter (charity sponsor details, who sells
  concessions, street addresses inside an alert); abstract summary sentences ("the kind of
  event that reminds you why…"); hype with no backing ("premier," "world-class," "legendary,"
  "curated experiences"); a closing line that repeats the opener; the "and honestly that is
  the point" shape.
- **Keep:** concrete specifics: dates, prices, capacity, ages, "over 130 artists," "sold out Saturday."
- "Pro move" tips only when sourced (official parking, documented timing). Never invent local knowledge.
- Opinions we hold: gymnastics is the best thing in the world; the Menlo Park park scene is
  excellent; ruthless about lame events.

Rules for the template:
- Subject line format never changes: `📅 THE WEEKEND SURVIVAL GUIDE: <Mon> <Fri>-<Sun>`
  (`Oct 30-Nov 1` across months).
- Lineup = the best in-weekend items (usually 4–8). Everything else that clears the bar
  goes in ⚡ This Weekend.
- Every card has When, Where, Cost and a link to the organizer's own page.
- Day headers show only days with cards. A multi-day event gets its card under its first day,
  plus one line under each later day's header: *Still on today: **Belmont Greek Festival** (see Saturday).*
- Age emojis: 🍼 toddlers/preschool, 🧒 elementary, 🤘 teens, 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 whole family.
- Clean Markdown that pastes straight into Substack.
