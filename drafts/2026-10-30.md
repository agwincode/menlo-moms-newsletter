### FRIDAY, OCT 30

---

**2026 Halloween Concert** 🧒 🤘 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:halloween-concert -->

**When:** 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM
**Where:** Dinkelspiel Auditorium, 471 Lagunita Dr, Stanford

The Stanford Symphony Orchestra and Wind Symphony play *Star Wars*, *Star Trek*, Holst's "Mars" and "Jupiter," and "Golden" from *KPop Demon Hunters*. Come in costume: the costume-contest winner conducts the final piece. $32 general, $27 seniors and non-Stanford students. It's always well attended, so buy ahead.

[Get tickets](https://events.stanford.edu/event/2026-halloween-concert)

---

### SATURDAY, OCT 31

---

**Helicopter Pumpkin Drop and Halloween Kids' Festival** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:pumpkin-drop -->

**When:** 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (pumpkin drop at noon)
**Where:** Hiller Aviation Museum, 601 Skyway Rd, San Carlos

Carnival games, model trains and a Great Pumpkin hunt through the Haunted Hangar, then a helicopter drops a pumpkin at noon. Included with museum admission; members free.

[Details](https://www.hiller.org/event/pumpkin-drop/)

---

**Halloween Tree Planting with Canopy** 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:canopy-tree-planting -->

**When:** 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
**Where:** Meet at Greenmeadow Community Center, 303 Parkside Dr, Palo Alto

Plant street trees with Canopy. Costumes encouraged; closed-toe shoes required. Sign up ahead.

[Sign up](https://canopy.org/get-involved/volunteer-signup/)

---

**Service Learning: Winter Clean Up at Filoli** 🧒 🤘 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:filoli-winter-cleanup -->

**When:** 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
**Where:** Filoli, 86 Cañada Rd, Woodside

Mulch, weed and clear paths with Filoli's horticulture team. Free, and volunteers get free admission to Filoli for the day. Reservation required.

[Reserve](https://filoli.org/whats-on/events/service-learning-winter-clean-up/)

---

**Halloween Night at the Museum + Flashlight Ghost Hunt** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:moah-halloween -->

**When:** 5:00 PM - 9:00 PM
**Where:** Museum of American Heritage, 351 Homer Ave, Palo Alto

Candy, a flashlight ghost hunt, mad-scientist demos, tarot readings and odd artifacts from MOAH's collection. Free; costumes welcome; RSVP on the event page.

[RSVP](https://www.moah.org/moahevents/halloween-night-at-the-museum-1)
