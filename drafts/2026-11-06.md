### SATURDAY, NOV 7

---

**History in Motion: Grand Opening at the San Mateo County History Museum** 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:history-in-motion -->

**When:** 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (new exhibits open at 11)
**Where:** San Mateo County History Museum, 2200 Broadway, Redwood City
**Cost:** TK

The museum in the old 1910 courthouse is unveiling three new venues and six new exhibits in one go. Music on Courthouse Square kicks things off at 10, the ribbon cutting is at 10:30, then it's kids' crafts, performances and pop-up talks until 2. Rain or shine.

[Details](https://historysmc.org/event/history-in-motion/)

---

### SUNDAY, NOV 8

---

**Día de los Muertos on Courthouse Square** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:dia-de-los-muertos-rwc:3 -->

**When:** TK
**Where:** Courthouse Square, Broadway between Hamilton St and Middlefield Rd, Redwood City
**Cost:** TK

Casa Círculo Cultural and the History Museum's celebration is one of the most colorful days on the Peninsula. This year's theme is Tlaxcala, a region of Mexico known for its Indigenous heritage. Expect community altars (there's an altar contest and a trunk-ofrenda contest), performers, food vendors and face painting.

**Heads up:** The performer lineup and schedule go up on the organizer's site closer to the day.

[Details](https://casacirculocultural.org/adult-initiative/dia-de-los-muertos/)
