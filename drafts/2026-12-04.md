Friday is the big one: Menlo Park lights its oak and kicks off the city's Centennial, while Palo Alto, San Carlos and Half Moon Bay all flip the switch the same night. Hanukkah starts at sundown on Friday too, so the weekend fills up with menorah lightings, latkes and sufganiyot. Pace yourselves.

## 🌟 The Weekend Lineup

*🍼 Toddlers & Preschool · 🧒 Elementary · 🤘 Teens · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Whole Family*

---

### FRIDAY, DEC 4

---

**Light Up the Season and the Centennial Kickoff** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:mp-light-up-the-season:1 -->

**When:** 5:30 PM - 7:00 PM
**Where:** Fremont Park, Santa Cruz Ave at University Dr, Menlo Park
**Cost:** Free

Menlo Park's Centennial year starts downtown with a winter wonderland: youth performances, Centennial photo spots, hot cocoa and the tree lighting. This year the city is lighting the big oak and giving the pine a well-deserved rest. Stay for dinner on Santa Cruz Ave and make a night of it.

**Heads up:** Hot cocoa is first come, while it lasts. The event runs rain or shine.

[Details](https://www.menlopark.gov/Citywide-calendar/Community-events/20261204-Light-Up-the-Season)

---

**Night of Lights in Half Moon Bay** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:hmb-night-of-lights:1 -->

**When:** 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM
**Where:** Mac Dutra Plaza and Main Street, Half Moon Bay
**Cost:** Free

The coast does tree lighting night with a whole parade. The holiday market opens at 4 on Kelly Ave, the tree lights at 5, karaoke follows, and the lighted parade rolls down Main Street around 6:15. Santa lands back at Mac Dutra Plaza at about 7:30 to hear wishes.

**Pro move:** The organizers say to arrive early and claim a spot along the parade route.

[Details](https://www.visithalfmoonbay.org/events/night-lights)

---

**Once Upon a Garden: Through the Looking Glass at Gamble Garden** 🍼 🧒

<!-- photos:gamble-garden-winter:1 -->

**When:** 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
**Where:** Gamble Garden, 1431 Waverley St, Palo Alto
**Cost:** $45, $36 for members

The family evening of Gamble Garden's Winter Festival: a Through the Looking Glass woodland garden, photos with Santa and his sleigh, storytime and refreshments under the Big Top, and kids' crafts. Adults get a glass of champagne. The festival's holiday marketplace (30+ vendors) is also open Friday and Saturday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM: $10, kids 12 and under free.

[Get tickets](https://www.gamblegarden.org/winter/winter-festival-2026/)

*Also Friday: tree lightings in **Palo Alto** and **San Carlos**, plus **First Friday Nights** at CuriOdyssey (see This Weekend).*

---

### SATURDAY, DEC 5

---

**Springline's Winter Wonderland** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:springline-holiday:1 -->

**When:** 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM
**Where:** Springline, 1300 El Camino Real, Menlo Park
**Cost:** Free

Springline's 5th annual holiday party brings in a real snow playground, so yes, the kids can build a snowman on El Camino. There's a complimentary photo with Santa, holiday card making, face painting, a family-friendly silent disco and The Freshmakers on the main stage from 6 to 8. Andytown is pouring hot chocolate.

[RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/springlines-free-5th-annual-holiday-celebration-tickets-2004049044640)

*Still on today: Gamble Garden's holiday marketplace (see Friday).*

---

### SUNDAY, DEC 6

---

**Latkepalooza: Hanukkah in the Village** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:latkepalooza:1 -->

**When:** 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM
**Where:** Peninsula Jewish Community Center, 800 Foster City Blvd, Foster City
**Cost:** Tickets from $12.51 on Eventbrite

The PJCC turns its Hanukkah party into an Eastern European village: art making, old-fashioned games, a magic show, live klezmer to dance to, fresh latkes, soup and sufganiyot. Everyone gathers under a big painted canopy at 4:40 for the candle lighting. People of all faiths are welcome, and parking is free.

[Get tickets](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/latkepalooza-hanukkah-in-the-village-tickets-2002763001049)

---

**Chanukah Festival and Grand Menorah Lighting** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:chabad-midpen-chanukah -->

**When:** 4:00 PM
**Where:** Courthouse Square, 2200 Broadway, Redwood City
**Cost:** Free; RSVP encouraged

Chabad MidPen's 17th annual festival takes over Courthouse Square with a giant snow globe, a juggling show and the lighting of the big menorah. Bring a new, unwrapped toy for the Lucile Packard Children's Hospital toy drive.

[RSVP](https://www.chabadmidpen.com/templates/articlecco_cdo/aid/3798612/jewish/Chanukah-Festival.htm)

## ⚡ This Weekend

- **Palo Alto Tree Lighting** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday, 5:30 PM - 7:00 PM (Lytton Plaza, Palo Alto). 🎄 School of Rock, holiday dancers and the Palo Alto Chamber Orchestra, plus winter treats. Free. [Details](https://www.paloalto.gov/Events-Directory/Community-Services/Holiday-Tree-Lighting)
- **Night of Holiday Lights** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday, 5:00 PM - 8:30 PM (Laurel Street, San Carlos). ❄️ San Carlos' 16th annual block party, with snow, crafts, a holiday show and Santa and the Snow Queen. Free. [Details](https://www.cityofsancarlos.org/residents/community-events/night-of-holiday-lights)
- **First Friday Nights at CuriOdyssey** 🍼 🧒: Friday, 4:30 PM - 8:00 PM (CuriOdyssey, San Mateo). ✨ The science museum stays open after dark to celebrate 10 years of IlluminOdyssey, with glow sticks, food and painting with light. [Details](https://curiodyssey.org/illuminodyssey10/)
- **Festival of Lights Hanukkah Party** 🍼: Sunday, 3:30 PM - 5:00 PM (Congregation Beth Am, Los Altos Hills). 🕎 Jewish Baby Network's party for babies and toddlers, with songs, puppets, crafts and a family menorah lighting. Bring a menorah. Free; registration appreciated, not required. [Register](https://jewishbabynetwork.org/peninsula/events/festival-of-lights-hanukkah-party-5/)
- **Hanukkah Lights on Ice** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Sunday, 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Burlingame Town Square). 🕎 A menorah lighting with a gelt drop and live music. Free. [Details](http://chabadnp.com/chanukah)
- **Holidays at Filoli** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: daily (Woodside). 🌟 Decorated house and garden by day (10:00 AM - 3:30 PM, from $40), lights after dark (from $45), kids 4 and under free. Timed tickets. [Tickets](https://filoli.org/holidays/)
- **Great Dickens Christmas Fair** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Cow Palace, Daly City). 🎩 Victorian London, two weekends left after this one. $56.56 adults and $35.86 youth with fees, under 5 free. [Tickets](https://dickensfair.com/tickets/)
