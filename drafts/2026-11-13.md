### FRIDAY, NOV 13

---

**San Mateo on Ice: Holiday Tree Lighting and Opening Night** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:san-mateo-on-ice -->

**When:** 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
**Where:** Fitzgerald Field, Central Park, 5th & El Camino Real, San Mateo
**Cost:** Free

The tree lighting and the rink's opening night in one go, with a figure skating show from Peninsula Skating Club performers including Olympic skater Polina Edmunds. Little skating fans will be glued to the boards. The rink opens to the public Saturday and runs daily, holidays included, through Sunday, Jan 10.

[Details](https://www.cityofsanmateo.org/Calendar/Home/SingleEvent?eventID=8086)

**Friends of the Menlo Park Library Book Sale** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:mp-book-sale -->

**When:** Friday 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM and Saturday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
**Where:** Menlo Park Library, 800 Alma St, Menlo Park
**Cost:** Free to browse; most items $1 or $2

Thousands of items, including children's books, board games and puzzles, and nearly everything is a dollar or two. Hand each kid a few bucks and let them pick their own stack. Every dollar goes back into library programs.

[Details](https://www.menlopark.gov/Government/Departments/Library-and-Community-Services/Events/Events-for-adults/20261113-14-FOL-Book-Sale)

### SATURDAY, NOV 14

---

**Holidays at Filoli: Opening Weekend** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:filoli-holiday-daytime:2 -->

**When:** Holiday Daytime 10:00 AM - 3:30 PM; Holiday Lights 4:00 PM - 10:00 PM (both start Saturday)
**Where:** Filoli, 86 Cañada Rd, Woodside
**Cost:** Daytime tickets start at $40, Holiday Lights at $45; daytime is free for members

New this year: the Filoli Family Farm in Thistlewyck Village, with mini cows, goats, sheep and a mother donkey with her baby, all included in daytime admission. After dark, Holiday Lights fills 16 acres of gardens with 28 miles of lights and a 210-foot light tunnel, plus the famous hot cocoa. All ages welcome at both.

**Pro move:** Filoli says daytime availability is limited, so reserve before you go.

[Get tickets](https://filoli.org/whats-on/events/holiday-daytime/)

---

**IlluminOdyssey 10 Opens at CuriOdyssey** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:illuminodyssey:1 -->

**When:** 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM (winter hours), Nov 14 through Jan 24
**Where:** CuriOdyssey, Coyote Point, San Mateo
**Cost:** Included with admission: $29.95 adults, $24.95 kids 18 months-17, free under 18 months and for members

The glowing light-and-science exhibit turns 10 this year, and every installation explores how light works. It's a darkened hall, which makes it a solid rainy-day plan, and the same ticket covers the rest of CuriOdyssey.

**Heads up:** Tickets are for a set date and time, so book online ahead.

[Get tickets](https://curiodyssey.org/illuminodyssey10/)

---

**Diwali Downtown** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:diwali-downtown-san-carlos -->

**When:** 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM
**Where:** Laurel Street, downtown San Carlos

The Festival of Lights comes to Laurel Street with dance from Kathakaars School of Dance students, San Carlos Sitaras, Twisha's Twirlers and The Koothadis, plus music, crafts, free henna, a storytime with author Sonali Patodia and bites from local chefs. Exactly the kind of community celebration we love.

[Details](https://www.cityofsancarlos.org/business_detail_T10_R450.php)
