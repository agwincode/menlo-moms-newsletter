Thanksgiving is next week, so a quick note of gratitude first: the Coats for Kids bin at the Arrillaga Family Recreation Center takes new and gently used coats through Friday, Nov 20, if you've got an outgrown jacket by the door. Then the holiday season opens wide: Victorian London pops up in Daly City, Santa starts his weekend shifts in Half Moon Bay and Palo Alto celebrates Diwali on Sunday.

🏈 **Big Game Saturday: Stanford at Cal, 4:30 PM PT in Berkeley, on ACC Network.** Make plans to watch.

## 🌟 The Weekend Lineup

### SATURDAY, NOV 21

---

**Great Dickens Christmas Fair: Opening Weekend** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:dickens-fair -->

**When:** 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Saturday and Sunday
**Where:** Cow Palace, Daly City
**Cost:** $50 adults (13+), $30 ages 5-12, under 5 free (plus fees)

The Cow Palace turns into lamplit Victorian London, with music halls, dance floors, shops full of handmade gifts and a Children's Tour of London for the kids that starts with a miniature steam train ride. It runs weekends through Dec 20, plus the Friday after Thanksgiving.

**Pro move:** Twilight tickets (entry after 3 PM) are $35 adults and $20 kids, if you just want a few hours.

[Get tickets](https://dickensfair.com/tickets/)

---

**Little Red Riding Hood at Palo Alto Children's Theatre** 🍼

<!-- photos:pact-little-red -->

**When:** 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM, Saturday and Sunday
**Where:** Palo Alto Children's Theatre, 1305 Middlefield Rd, Palo Alto
**Cost:** $20

PACT's Playhouse Series is story-teller style theatre made for ages two to six: simple sets, about 55 minutes, and lots of chances to get up and wiggle. The perfect first show for a toddler.

**Heads up:** The 10 AM shows and Sunday's 2 PM are sold out. The noon shows still had seats on Friday.

[Get tickets](https://pact.showare.com/eventperformances.asp?evt=9)

---

**Santa Arrives at Santa's Tree Farm** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:santas-tree-farm:2 -->

**When:** Santa 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM; train, gifts and Mrs. Claus' kitchen 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Saturday and Sunday)
**Where:** Santa's Tree Farm and Village, Half Moon Bay
**Cost:** Santa visits free with any tree purchase; Storytime with Santa or Buddy the Elf $14 per child; Reindeer Talk with Rudolph $10 per child

The 52nd season at this 487-acre choose-and-cut farm, and this is Santa's first weekend in the village. Ride the Santa Express, grab a treat in Mrs. Claus' kitchen and saw down your own tree (up to 13 feet). Santa's here weekends through Dec 13, plus the Friday after Thanksgiving.

[Details](https://www.santastreefarm.com/)

*Still on today: **Little Red Riding Hood** and **Santa's Tree Farm** (see Saturday).*

---

### SUNDAY, NOV 22

---

**Festival of Lights: A Diwali Celebration** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:pa-diwali:1 -->

**When:** 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
**Where:** Mitchell Park Community Center, 3700 Middlefield Rd, Palo Alto
**Cost:** Free

The Palo Alto library's Diwali party has henna with teaching artist Rachel-Anne Palacios, rangoli chalk art in the courtyard, crafts, and a Noorani Dance performance from 2:30 to 3:30. Easy, free and a lovely way to spend a Sunday afternoon.

**Heads up:** The henna line closes at 3:30, and everything runs while supplies last, so arrive early.

[Details](https://www.paloalto.gov/Events-Directory/Library/Festival-of-Lights-A-Diwali-Celebration)

*Still on today: **Great Dickens Christmas Fair**, **Little Red Riding Hood** and **Santa's Tree Farm** (see Saturday).*
