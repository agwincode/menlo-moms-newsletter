Happy Thanksgiving! We're grateful for every one of you who reads, forwards and shows up, and for the city staff, librarians and volunteers who keep this town's calendar so full. The long weekend is the official start of the holiday season around here: gardens and museums are glowing, San Jose turns on its trees and the SFCO brings its puppet Nutcracker back to Palo Alto.

### FRIDAY, NOV 27

---

**Christmas in the Park: Opening Night** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:christmas-in-the-park:2 -->

**When:** Opens 6:00 PM Friday; then Friday-Sunday noon - 10:00 PM, Monday-Thursday noon - 9:00 PM, through Jan 3
**Where:** Plaza de San José, Market St between San Fernando and San Carlos, San Jose
**Cost:** Free

San Jose's holiday tradition since 1980 kicks off its 47th year on Friday night: a downtown park full of decorated trees, Christmas light displays and live entertainment. Santa's House is open too, and visits and photos with Santa are free (donations welcome).

**Heads up:** Santa's hours aren't posted yet; the park says they'll go up on its Events page.

[Details](https://christmasinthepark.com/)

---

**Holidays at Filoli** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

<!-- photos:filoli-holidays:1 -->

**When:** Holiday Daytime 10:00 AM - 3:30 PM (last entry 1:30 PM); Holiday Lights 4:00 PM - 10:00 PM (last entry 8:00 PM). Runs through Jan 10.
**Where:** Filoli, 86 Cañada Rd, Woodside
**Cost:** Daytime from $40; Holiday Lights from $45; members free with a reservation

Daytime tickets include the decorated house and garden, Thistlewyck Village and the new Family Farm, with mini cows, goats, sheep and donkeys plus games at the Elf Academy. Come back after dark for the lights in the garden. A long weekend with nowhere to be is exactly when we'd go.

**Heads up:** Tickets are timed and non-refundable, and Filoli warns that weekends and holidays may sell out. Book ahead.

[Get tickets](https://filoli.org/holidays/)

---

**IlluminOdyssey 10 at CuriOdyssey** 🍼 🧒

<!-- photos:illuminodyssey:2 -->

**When:** 9:30 AM - 4:30 PM daily through Jan 24 (closed Thanksgiving Day)
**Where:** CuriOdyssey, 1651 Coyote Point Dr, San Mateo
**Cost:** Included with admission: $29.95 adults, $24.95 ages 18 months-17, under 18 months free

The science museum's glowing holiday exhibit turns 10 this year, with 10 new installations on how light works. It's indoors, so it's a great rainy-day plan, and kids can go straight from the light tables to the animals and the rest of the museum.

**Pro move:** Timed tickets are recommended (not required), so reserve a slot for the long weekend.

[Get tickets](https://curiodyssey.org/illuminodyssey10/)

*Also Friday: **Green Friday** free park day and Santa's first weekday shift at **Santa's Tree Farm** (see This Weekend).*

### SUNDAY, NOV 29

---

**Nutcracker "Sweet" with The Puppet Company** 🍼 🧒

<!-- photos:sfco-nutcracker-sweet:3 -->

**When:** 4:00 PM (doors open at 3:00 PM)
**Where:** First United Methodist Church, Fellowship Hall, 625 Hamilton Ave, Palo Alto
**Cost:** Free

The San Francisco Chamber Orchestra plays highlights from Tchaikovsky's ballet while Oakland Fairyland's Puppet Company performs it with a miniature puppet cast, plus a fire-breathing dragon, confetti and a show-and-tell of the puppets. It's about 40 minutes, made for young audiences, and the SFCO calls it a Thanksgiving weekend tradition.

**Heads up:** Open seating, first come, first served, until the hall is full. RSVPs aren't required but help the orchestra plan.

[RSVP](https://www.thesfco.org/familyconcerts)

*Still on today: **Holidays at Filoli**, **IlluminOdyssey** and **Christmas in the Park** (see Friday).*

## ⚡ This Weekend

- **Great Dickens Christmas Fair** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday, Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Cow Palace, Daly City). 🎩 The Friday after Thanksgiving is one of the fair's bonus days. $50 adults (13+), $30 ages 5-12, under 5 free, plus fees; twilight entry after 3 PM is $35 and $20. Tickets online. [Tickets](https://dickensfair.com/tickets/)
- **Santa at Santa's Tree Farm** 🍼 🧒 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday, Saturday and Sunday; Santa 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM, train, gifts and Mrs. Claus' kitchen 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM on the weekend (Half Moon Bay). 🎅 Friday is Santa's only weekday at the farm this season. Visits are complimentary with any purchase. [Details](https://www.santastreefarm.com/)
- **Green Friday** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday (East Bay Regional Parks). 🌳 Entrance and parking fees are waived at every East Bay regional park on the day after Thanksgiving, including Ardenwood Historic Farm in Fremont, just over the Dumbarton Bridge. [Details](https://www.ebparks.org/we-celebrate/green-friday)
- **Santa Cruz Holiday Lights Train** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦: Friday and Saturday, departures 5:00 PM and 6:30 PM (Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk). 🚂 An hour-long ride in vintage cars covered in lights, with live holiday music to sing along to. $58.25 adults and $38.11 kids in standard cars, under 2 free, and each ticket includes a $10 Boardwalk card. [Tickets](https://roaringcamp.com/holiday-lights)
